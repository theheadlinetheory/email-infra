💬 Held: positive replies

These burned inboxes were kept in their campaign because a prospect replied positively to them. A reply lives in the mailbox that sent it and is welded to it by message-id — SmartLead's Reallocate mailboxes only redistributes the unsent lead queue and can never move an existing thread, so detaching would freeze the conversation ("the mailbox is disconnected"). The replacement inbox already went in, so the campaign has its capacity back. These are also excluded from the cancel list — cancelling deletes the mailbox and the thread with it, permanently. Work the reply, then Remove from campaign.

🔥 Burned domains — reallocate & cancel

loading…
Top priority. Inboxes burned (bounce ≥3% & reply ≤0.5%) for 3 straight days while actively sending, grouped by domain — Zapmail bills by whole domain, so decisions are made per domain. Reallocate swaps burned inboxes out of their campaign for niche-matched reserve (frontend only, no code). Cancel domain schedules Zapmail removal on the next billing date and registers it with the #zapmail-billing bot. Burned inboxes whose campaign is paused/completed are idle — they show in All inboxes, not here.

⚡ Acquisition sending capacity

loading…
Every acquisition inbox we pay for, and whether it is actually pushing mail. Capacity is the sum of each inbox's real SmartLead daily cap. Deployed is the slice pointed at a live campaign that still has leads queued. Stranded is the expensive one — inboxes sitting in an ACTIVE campaign that has run out of leads: SmartLead never releases them, so they look busy and send nothing. Pick idle inboxes below, choose a campaign, and move them. Removing a sender from a live campaign that still has leads needs an explicit override, and a live campaign can never be taken to zero senders.
What the five states mean every acquisition inbox is in exactly one of these
StateWhat it meansPlain EnglishWhat to do
Sending In a live campaign that still has leads Working normally. Mail is going out today. Nothing — leave it alone.
Stranded In a live campaign with 0 leads left The expensive one. The campaign is still switched ON but has run out of people to email. SmartLead does not hand the inbox back, so it looks busy in SmartLead while sending nothing. You would never spot this by looking at the campaign list. Free it and put it on a campaign that still has leads. Safe — there is nothing left for it to send.
Parked Only in paused / completed campaigns The campaign it belongs to was switched off or finished. Not sending, and honestly showing as not sending. Free reuse. Move it whenever you need senders.
Unassigned In no campaign at all Never attached to anything, or detached and never re-used. Pure spare stock. Use these first — nothing to detach from.
Blocked Burned, or SMTP disconnected Cannot send no matter which campaign you put it in. We are still paying for it. Moving it will not help. Replace or cancel it (Renewals & reserve tab).
"Idle" is not a sixth state. It is the shorthand for the three you can reclaim — Stranded Parked Unassigned — which is what the Idle / day figure and the Idle only filter both mean. Note: the All inboxes tab also has a status called "Idle", but that one answers a different question — whether an inbox is healthy enough to judge. An inbox can be Sending here and still show as low-volume there.
